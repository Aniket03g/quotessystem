📄 Understanding Quotes

A quote is a formal document showing products and pricing for a customer.

What's in a Quote?

  • Customer account information
  • Quote date and validity period
  • List of products with prices
  • Quantities and totals
  • Additional notes or terms
  • Quote status

Quote Statuses:

Draft - Being prepared
Sent - Sent to customer
Approved - Customer approved
Final - Completed

1
Go to Quotes Page

2
Click "Create Quote" Button

📝 Step 2: Select Account

1

2

Page 8

📝 Step 3: Set Quote Details

📅 Quote Date

• Defaults to today's date
• You can change if needed
• This is when the quote is created

⏰ Valid Until

• Defaults to 30 days from today
• Adjust based on your needs
• Quote expires after this date

📊 Quote Status

• Choose: Draft, Sent, Approved, or Final
• Usually start with "Draft"
• Update as quote progresses

🏷️ Quote Type

• Customer: Standard pricing
• Dealer: Wholesale pricing
• This affects product prices!

⚠️ Important: Quote Type determines pricing! Choose "Customer" for regular customers, "Dealer" for resellers.

🔄 Creating Quote Versions

Versions help you track changes and maintain quote history.

Why Create Versions?

  • Customer requests different products or quantities
  • Prices change
  • You want to offer multiple options
  • Keep history of negotiations
1
Open Original Quote
Go to the quote you want to create a version of
2
Click "Create Version"
System creates a copy linked to the original
3
Make Changes to New Version
Edit as needed - original remains unchanged

📦 Understanding Products & Pricing

Product Information

Each product in the catalog includes:
• Product Name and Description
• Brand and Model
• Product Code and HSN Code
• Tax Rate
• Two price tiers (Customer & Dealer)

💰 Customer Pricing

  • Standard retail price
  • Use for regular customers
  • Higher profit margin
  • Default for most quotes

🏪 Dealer Pricing

  • Wholesale price
  • Use for resellers/dealers
  • Lower price point
  • Bulk purchase pricing

✨ Best Practices for Success

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong Quote Type

Problem: Selecting "Dealer" for regular customer or vice versa
Solution: Always verify customer type before selecting quote type

❌ Incorrect Quantities

Problem: Entering wrong product quantities
Solution: Confirm quantities with customer before creating quote

❌ Forgetting to Update Status

Problem: Quote status doesn't reflect actual state
Solution: Update status as quote progresses through workflow

🔧 Quick Troubleshooting

Can't Find an Account?

• Make sure you're spelling the name correctly
• Try searching with partial name
• Contact admin if account doesn't exist

Product Not Showing Price?

• Check if you selected the quote type first
• Verify product has pricing in catalog
• Refresh the page and try again

Quote Won't Save?

• Ensure all required fields are filled (marked with *)
• Check that account is selected
• Verify at least one product is added
• Make sure dates are valid

PDF Not Generating?

• Ensure quote is saved first
• Check that all product details are complete
• Try refreshing the page
• Contact admin if problem persists
