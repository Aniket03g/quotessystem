Getting Started

Logging In

Open the CRM in your browser. You will see the login page.

  1. Enter your Email and Password provided by your administrator.
  2. Click Login.
  3. You will be taken to the Quotes page (or the Admin Dashboard if you are an admin).
ℹ️

If you see an error, double-check your email and password. Contact your admin if you cannot get in.

First Login — Changing Your Password

When your account is created by an admin, you will be given a temporary password. The system will ask you to change it immediately before you can use anything.

  1. Log in with the temporary password.
  2. You will be redirected to the Change Password page automatically.
  3. Enter your temporary password in Current Password.
  4. Choose a new password (minimum 8 characters) and confirm it.
  5. Click Change Password. You will be taken to the Quotes page.
⚠️

You cannot access any other part of the system until you change your temporary password.

Understanding Your Role

Regular User User

Can create and manage your own leads and those assigned to you. Create quotes, accounts, and contacts. Cannot access admin settings.

Administrator Admin

Full access to all leads across all users. Can create users, reset passwords, access dashboard, assign leads, and delete records.

💡

Not sure what role you have? Check the name shown in the top-right corner — admins see a Dashboard link and a User Management link in the sidebar.

Searching and filtering

  1. Click the + Create Lead button (top right of the leads page).
  2. You will be taken to the Create Lead form.
  3. Fill in the required fields (marked *) and any optional details.
  4. Click Save Lead.

Viewing a Lead

Click the View button (eye icon) on any lead row. A panel opens showing all lead details in a read-only format, including:

  • All contact details and address sections
  • Status and source badges
  • Assigned user, last contacted date, and next follow-up
  • Notes and requirements

Editing a Lead

  1. Find the lead in the list and click the Edit button (pencil icon).
  2. An edit panel opens with all the same fields as the create form.
  3. Make your changes and click Save.
ℹ️

Regular users cannot change the Assigned To field — that field is disabled for non-admins.

Converting a Lead to an Account

When a lead is ready to become a customer, you convert them into an Account.

  1. Open the lead (View or Edit).
  2. Click the Convert to Account button.
  3. A preview modal shows what account record will be created from the lead's data.
  4. Confirm the conversion.
  5. The lead status changes to Converted and a new Account is created automatically using the lead's details.
⚠️

The Convert button is only visible to the lead's creator or an admin.

Deleting a Lead

🔒

Admin only. Click the Delete button on a lead row. A confirmation dialog will appear — confirm to permanently delete the lead. This cannot be undone.

Quotes Management

Quotes are formal price proposals sent to accounts. The system supports versioning, PDF export, and dual branding (Grove Systems / GreenOCare).

Quotes List

Click My Quotes (or Quotes) in the sidebar.

ColumnDescription
#Quote number
Quote NameSubject/title of the quote
DateDate the quote was created
StatusCurrent status (Draft, Sent, Approved, etc.)
Total AmountCalculated total from all products
ActionsView detail, Download PDF

Use the search box to find a quote by name or account.

Creating a Quote

  1. Click + Create Quote.
  2. Search for and select the Account (customer). Account details auto-populate (company name, phone, address).
  3. Add Products — search for products and add them to the quote. Pricing is calculated automatically.
  4. Set the Status (typically start with Draft).
  5. Click Save to create the quote.

Quote Detail Page

Click a quote's name or the View button to open the full detail page.

What you'll find here

  • Quote Subject and date
  • Status badge — Draft, Sent, Approved
  • Sub Total and Valid Until date
  • Account Information — company name, address, phone (with a link to the account)
  • Products — list of all products in this quote with quantities and prices
  • Version History — timeline showing all versions with their statuses

Actions available

  • Edit Products — add/remove/change products and quantities
  • New Version — create a new version of this quote (e.g. after revising prices)
  • Preview PDF — see what the PDF will look like before downloading
  • Download PDF — export the quote as a PDF
  • Logo Selector — choose between Grove Systems or GreenOCare branding for the PDF

Exporting a Quote as PDF

  1. Open the quote detail page.
  2. Select the logo / brand you want on the PDF (Grove Systems or GreenOCare).
  3. Click Preview PDF to check it first, or click Download PDF directly.
  4. The PDF downloads to your computer, named after the quote.
💡

Always preview the PDF before sending it to a customer to make sure the branding and product list look correct.

Quote Versioning

Every time you revise a quote, you can create a new version instead of overwriting the original. This keeps a full history of what was proposed and when.

  • The Version selector at the top of the detail page lets you switch between versions.
  • The Version History timeline shows each version with a status dot: Latest, Sent, Approved.
  • Click New Version to start a revised quote based on the current one.

Creating a new user

  1. Click + Create User.
  2. Enter the user's Name, Email, and select a Role (User or Admin).
  3. Click Create. The system generates a temporary password.
  4. Share the temporary password with the new user — they will be forced to change it on first login.
⚠️

Copy and save the temporary password immediately — it is only shown once after creation.

Resetting a User's Password

If a user forgets their password or is locked out:

  1. Go to User Management.
  2. Find the user in the list.
  3. Click Reset Password in the Actions column.
  4. A new temporary password is generated and displayed. Share it with the user.
  5. The user will be required to change this temporary password on their next login.

Managing All Leads (Admin View)

As an admin, the Leads list shows all leads from all users — not just your own. This lets you:

  • See the full pipeline across your team
  • Filter by Assigned To to see any individual team member's leads
  • Reassign leads by editing the Assigned To field
  • Delete any lead (with confirmation)
  • Convert any lead to an account

Tips & Common Questions

General tips

  • Overdue follow-ups appear in red on the leads list — address these first.
  • Use the Notes / Requirements field on leads to capture anything said in calls or meetings.
  • When creating a quote, always double-check the Account is correct before adding products — account details auto-populate and affect the PDF.
  • If a product you need is not in the system, contact your admin — the catalogue is managed externally.
  • Billing and Shipping addresses on leads and accounts have a "Same as Billing" checkbox to save time.

Common questions

Q: I can't see some leads — where are they?
A: Regular users only see leads they created or were assigned to. Ask an admin to assign leads to you, or ask them to check the full list.

Q: I can't change who a lead is assigned to.
A: The "Assigned To" field is restricted to admins only. Ask an admin to reassign it.

Q: How do I change my own password?
A: Currently, password changes happen automatically when you first log in with a temporary password. If you need to reset your password later, ask an admin to reset it for you — you will get a new temporary password to log in with.

Q: The Convert to Account button is not visible.
A: Only the lead's original creator or an admin can see the Convert button. If you need to convert a lead you did not create, ask the creator or an admin to do it.

Q: Can I edit products or their prices?
A: No — the product catalogue is read-only in the CRM. Contact your admin to update product details.
